COLOMBIA

A page about the beautiful South American country

Known for its coffee and rich cultural history. The beautiful country of Colombia has so much to offer to the world.

Colombia is located in the northwestern South America with the countries Panama, Venezuela, Ecuador, Peru, and Brazil bordering it. This 207 year old country is rich in culture and biodiversity. It is the only country in South America with coastlines on both the Pacific Ocean and Caribbean Sea.

Three facts about Colombia

  • Second most biodiverse country in the world. 1/10th of all species on Earth can be found in Colombia!
  • World's leading source of emerald exportation with 70-90% of the world's emeralds coming from Colombia.
  • The mythical city of gold and riches known as El Dorado was based on a group of indigenous peoples in Colombia.
